# INLS509_Midterm_Exam_Practice
A diagnostic and practice exam for INLS 509

I built INLS 509 Exam Lab, a practice-test page with written answers, no multiple choice. It has four tabs:

Diagnostic: 27 short questions covering every topic, from basic definitions and controlled vocabularies through query likelihood and comparing models. Write a sentence or two, then press Check and Claude grades it. Its feedback on math mistakes uses the plain-word recipe format. Or press Show answer and compare it yourself. Then mark each one Got it, Shaky, or Missed.

Cheat-sheet list: builds itself from the Diagnostic. Every question you mark Shaky or Missed adds a ready-to-copy line for your notes sheet, and a breakdown by topic shows where to spend your sheet space.

Exam A and Exam B: two full practice exams in last year’s 6-question, 100-point layout, with a 75-minute timer. Press Submit for grading and Claude scores every part, adds feedback, and shows the model answer under each part. The questions are new: different numbers, plus a couple of twists he hasn’t asked before, like QL with smoothing in a “same score?” question and TF-IDF scoring.
